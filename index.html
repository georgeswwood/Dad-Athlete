Key Reminders

Lower Days — 0545 Alarm

AM Mobility Layer means ~75 min total. Don't skip it to claw back time — front squat and back squat cold is where injuries happen.

First Two Weeks Are Adjustment

Morning sessions feel harder for the first 10–14 days. Circadian adjustment. By week 3 you'll wake near 0550 naturally. Judge at week 3, not week 1.

Wednesday Is Not a Day Off

60-second passive hang. Then 10 min hip flexor and thoracic mobility. This is what lets Friday's deadlift be heavy.

Mirror Is a Lagging Indicator

Track the lifts. When the numbers move, the physique change is locked in — takes 4–6 weeks to show. Trust the log.

Rest Periods Are Non-Negotiable

3 min compounds. 90 sec accessory. 45–60 sec isolation. Undisciplined rest is why sessions overrun.

Saturday's Dad Walk

30–45 min with Hudson. Zone 2 and active recovery. It's also the point of the programme. The physique serves the life.
